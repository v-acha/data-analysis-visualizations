# Data Analysis and Visualization

Projects that turn collected data into clear comparisons, interactive visualizations, and decisions people can act on. This repository includes the analysis and presentation of findings, with project-specific details in each folder.

## Featured project

### Grant Landscape and Growth Strategy for a Coastal Nonprofit

A client project for Pacifica Beach Coalition (PBC). The organization wanted to understand which grants comparable coastal and environmental nonprofits received, how its finances compared with peers, and where to focus its fundraising efforts.

I collected and analyzed publicly available nonprofit and grant information, then compared peer awards with PBC’s internal grant records. I delivered a three-tab Tableau dashboard and presented the findings and recommendations to the organization:

- **Overview:** PBC’s revenue, expenses, and staffing compared with peer organizations.
- **Grants:** Grantmakers, award amounts, recipient organizations, and opportunities identified through the peer comparison.
- **Recommendations:** Funding priorities, revenue diversification ideas, and staffing considerations informed by the analysis.
