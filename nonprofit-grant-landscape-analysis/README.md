# Grant Landscape and Growth Strategy for a Coastal Nonprofit

A client project for Pacifica Beach Coalition (PBC), a California coastal conservation nonprofit. PBC was planning for growth and wanted evidence to guide its fundraising and staffing decisions. I researched comparable nonprofits, analyzed the grants they received, and delivered an interactive Tableau dashboard and presentation to the organization.

## Business questions

- How does PBC’s financial position compare with organizations doing similar work?
- Which grantmakers have awarded grants to those peers, and how large were the awards?
- Which grantmakers appear among peer awards but have no recorded award to PBC in the analyzed data?
- What do peer funding models and staffing patterns suggest for PBC’s growth plans?

## Research and analysis

I collected and organized publicly available nonprofit profiles, financial information, and grant records. Sources used during the research included [GuideStar](https://www.guidestar.org/), [Cause IQ](https://www.causeiq.com/), [ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/), and [IRS Form 990 downloads](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-990-series-downloads).

I combined publicly available information on peer organizations and grant awards with PBC’s internal records to identify existing grantmaker relationships and potential new funding opportunities.

I compared organizations by mission, programs, geography, and financial scale. The analysis brought together revenue and expense measures, grant awards, grantmakers, and staffing information to show both how peers fund their work and where PBC could investigate new opportunities.

## Tableau dashboard

The dashboard has three tabs:

1. **Overview** compares PBC with peer organizations on grant revenue, other revenue, total revenue, expenses, and staffing. Viewers can examine individual peers and filter comparisons by fiscal year and revenue group.
2. **Grants** Grants compares grantmakers that funded PBC with those that funded similar organizations but had not awarded a grant to PBC, based on a comparison with PBC’s internal records. It also shows grant amounts and recipient organizations.
3. **Recommendations** brings the analysis together into potential actions: grantmakers to research, ways to diversify revenue, and fundraising roles and salary benchmarks to consider.

**[Explore the Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/vanellsa.acha/viz/PBC_17364557135820/Overview)**

## Delivery and business use

I presented the dashboard and an accompanying presentation to the organization. Together, they gave PBC a way to review its position against peers, investigate grantmakers already supporting similar work, and discuss how its fundraising and staffing approach could support growth.

The recommendations were research priorities, not guarantees of funding. A peer’s grant award does not establish PBC’s eligibility or confirm that a grant program is currently accepting applications; those details require direct verification with the grantmaker.
